Privacy,
plainly.

Lexnetic Solutions · ABN 52 645 217 449 · Footscray VIC · Updated 17 Nov 2026

What we collect

What you put in the quote request: business name, suburb, your name, phone, the service you need and any notes. Your email address comes with the email you send us.

Why

To arrange a quote for you from one service provider, and to follow up on that request.

Who sees it

One commercial cleaning or waste provider that fits your request, and only if you ticked the consent box. We don't sell, rent or trade your details. We may receive a referral fee from the provider if you become their customer.

Marketing

We only send you tips or updates if you ticked the separate optional box. Every message has an easy way to opt out.

This website

The quote form doesn't store anything on this site. It opens an email to us from your own email app. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

Storage

Requests are kept in our Google (Gmail) account and deleted 12 months after your request. If you become a client through us, we keep only what our tax and business records require.

Your rights

Email lexnetic@gmail.com to see, correct or delete your details. We'll respond within 30 days.

Complaints

Contact us first. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
