Privacy note

Lexnetic Solutions (ABN 52 645 217 449), Footscray VIC. Last updated 17 November 2026.

What we collect

The details you enter in the quote request form: business name, suburb, your name, email, phone, and information about the service you need.

Why

To arrange a quote for you from one service provider, and to follow up on that request.

Who we share it with

One commercial cleaning or waste provider that matches your request, and only if you ticked the consent box on the form. We do not sell, rent or trade your details. We may receive a referral fee from the provider if you become their customer.

Marketing

We only send you tips or updates if you ticked the separate optional box. Every message has an easy way to opt out.

Storage and retention

Requests are stored in our Google account. We delete them 12 months after your request, unless you become a client through us, in which case we keep only what we need for our tax and business records.

Access, correction, deletion

Email lexnetic@gmail.com to see, correct or delete your details. We'll respond within 30 days.

Complaints

Contact us first at the email above. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
