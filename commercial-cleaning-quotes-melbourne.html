Cleaning quotes:
what to ask.

A practical checklist for Melbourne offices, warehouses, clinics and venues getting commercial cleaning quotes.

Before you ask for quotes

Quotes are only comparable if every cleaner is pricing the same job. Write down these basics first. It takes ten minutes and saves weeks of back-and-forth.

  • Floor area and layout: rough m², number of floors, kitchens, bathrooms and meeting rooms.
  • Frequency: how many cleans a week, and which days.
  • Time window: before opening, after close, or during business hours.
  • Floor types: carpet, hard floors, or a mix. These change the equipment and time needed.
  • Access: keys, swipe cards, alarm codes and any building induction.
  • Extras: windows, carpet steam-cleaning, consumables (paper, soap, bin liners), restocking.

The scope of works

Ask every cleaner for a written scope of works: a task list showing what's done each visit, weekly and monthly. Most disputes start because "general clean" meant different things to each side.

Questions to ask every cleaner

  • Do you hold public liability insurance, and can you send a certificate of currency?
  • Are cleaners employees or subcontractors, and are they paid under the Cleaning Services Award?
  • Will the same team clean our site each time?
  • Do staff have police checks, which matter for medical, childcare and secure sites?
  • How do you check quality, and how fast do you fix a missed clean?
  • What's the notice period to end the contract, and is there a minimum term?
  • Are consumables included, or charged on top?
  • How are price increases handled during the contract?

Comparing quotes fairly

Put the quotes side by side against your scope. A cheap quote that drops tasks, or allows fewer hours on site, often costs more in complaints and re-cleans. Ask each provider how many hours per visit they've allowed. It's the quickest way to spot an unrealistic price.

Red flags

  • No written scope or contract.
  • Can't show insurance.
  • A price that's far below every other quote with no explanation.
  • Long lock-in terms with automatic renewal.
