FAQ

Fair questions.

Is it really free?+

Yes. You never pay us. If you become a provider's customer, the provider may pay us a referral fee. That doesn't change the price they quote you.

Who gets my details?+

One provider that fits your request, and only if you ticked the consent box. We don't sell or rent contact details. See the privacy note.

Which areas do you cover?+

Metro Melbourne. Tell us the suburb and we'll confirm whether we have a provider that services it.

What if I don't like the quote?+

Then you don't take it. There's no obligation, and nothing is owed to us or the provider.

Are small jobs OK?+

For waste and recycling, any size is fine. For cleaning, our providers are best suited to regular cleans, once a week or more. Ask anyway, and we'll tell you honestly if we can't help.
