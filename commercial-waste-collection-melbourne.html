Business waste:
what to know.

How business waste and recycling collection is priced, what to prepare, and the questions that avoid surprise charges.

Know your streams

Commercial waste is priced per bin and per collection ("lift"). The more you separate, the less goes in the most expensive general-waste bin. Common streams:

  • General waste (landfill)
  • Mixed recycling: plastics, cans, paper
  • Cardboard: big volumes for retail, warehouses and bottle shops
  • Glass: a big one for hospitality
  • Food organics: cafés, restaurants, commercial kitchens
  • Specialised: e-waste, grease traps, confidential paper, medical waste (licensed handling)

Before you ask for quotes

Write down: which streams you need, roughly how full each bin gets per week, bin sizes if you know them, how the truck gets to your bins (laneway, loading dock, basement), and any time restrictions on collection.

What drives the price

  • Bin size and number of lifts per week.
  • Access: tight laneways or basements can need smaller trucks or extra handling.
  • Bin rental on top of collection.
  • Contamination: the wrong items in recycling can trigger extra charges.
  • Surcharges: fuel or landfill levies passed through.

Questions to ask every provider

  • Is the price per lift, per week or per month, and what's included?
  • Are bin rental, fuel or levy surcharges extra?
  • What's the contract term, and does it renew automatically?
  • What happens if we need an extra lift, or fewer?
  • How do contamination charges work?
  • Can you supply a waste report showing what we divert from landfill?

New site? Book early

If you're opening a new venue or moving into a new office, organise waste collection before opening week. Bin delivery and the first collection schedule can take time to line up.
